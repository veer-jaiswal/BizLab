From the notebook

You don’t need a bigger network. You need a better question.

The conversations that change your work rarely begin with a pitch. They begin when you can name the uncertainty you are genuinely trying to resolve.

That was the lesson I missed early on: I showed up to collect answers, not to develop a point of view. The result was a lot of polite advice and very little usable insight.
