Why I write BizLab

Business, finance, economics, and venture capital shape careers, companies, and everyday money decisions, yet they're often explained in jargon that keeps newcomers out. Most people never get a plain-language walkthrough of how a balance sheet works, how startups get funded, or why prices move.

BizLab exists to close that gap. Each article explains one idea clearly, shows the math with simple examples, and points out what to watch for, so readers can make better decisions as students, founders, employees, or investors. I also share what I'm learning from working in venture capital and startups, because seeing these ideas up close is one of the best ways to understand them.

Everything here is for general education and is not financial, legal, or tax advice. Questions, corrections, or ideas for a topic? Send me a message.
